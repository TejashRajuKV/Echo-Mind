Powerful Features

Advanced AI-powered fact-checking with comprehensive analysis

Core Evidence & Verification Engine

Evidence Intelligence Engine

Retrieves verified fact-checks, primary official releases, and live news before reasoning — grounding every verdict in auditable evidence.

Multi-Source Conflict Detection

Detects consensus vs contradiction across independent publishers, calculating weighted convergence and controversy metrics.

Atomic Claim Decomposition

Breaks compound statements into verifiable sub-claims, isolating specific factual assertions for granular accuracy.

Echo-Chamber Deduplication

Detects syndicated wire copies (PTI, AP, Reuters) to eliminate duplicate reporting and prevent artificial consensus inflation.

Explainable Credibility Scoring

Transparent multi-factor formula evaluating source agreement, domain authority, and publisher diversity instead of black-box numbers.

Gamified Critical Thinking

Earn badges, track your verification journey with persistent sessions, and receive category-tailored educational tips.

Intelligence Extensions

Claim Evolution Tracker

Links repeated versions of the same claim across time — tracking how wording mutates, evidence accumulates, and verdicts drift across publications.

About Echo Mind

Echo Mind represents the cutting edge of AI-powered fact-checking technology by Thunderwing Falcons. Our prototype combines the power of Google's Vertex AI platform with Gemini 2.5 Pro models and BigQuery database to provide accurate, educational, and engaging fact-checking experiences.

Built for the modern information age, Echo Mind not only identifies misinformation but also educates users on how to become better critical thinkers through gamified learning and personalized insights.
